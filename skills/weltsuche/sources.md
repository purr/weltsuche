# where answers live

starting points by topic, for when the engines' results are not enough. most
have no engine in weltsuche: search them with `site:domain` (through `search`
or WebSearch), or `fetch` the site's own search page and then its pages.
a site marked walled refuses plain clients: reach it through `site:` on a
search engine instead. this list is a start, not a limit; the results of a
first search usually show which sites a field trusts.

| topic | sites |
|---|---|
| prices and products | engines: `["shopping"]` (amazon, ebay, kleinanzeigen), `airbnb`; no engine: idealo.de, geizhals.de / .at (de), kakaku.com (ja), smzdm.com (zh), danawa.com (ko), pricespy |
| electronic parts | the maker (ti.com, st.com, analog.com, microchip.com), octopart.com, alldatasheet.com, lcsc.com, mouser.com, digikey.com, eevblog.com (forum) |
| audio | audiosciencereview.com (measurements), head-fi.org, erji.net (zh), kakaku.com reviews (ja), ixbt.com (ru) |
| medicine | clinicaltrials.gov, cochranelibrary.com, dailymed.nlm.nih.gov (us labels), ema.europa.eu, gelbe-liste.de (de), rlsnet.ru (ru), dxy.cn (zh) |
| papers | semanticscholar.org, openalex.org, cyberleninka.ru (ru, open access), jstage.jst.go.jp (ja), cnki.net (zh) |
| patents, standards, law | patents.google.com, worldwide.espacenet.com, rfc-editor.org, eur-lex.europa.eu |
| software | the project's issues and discussions (`gh search issues "..."`), its docs and changelog, its discourse forum, lore.kernel.org (linux mailing lists), 4pda.to (ru, android) |
| what people report | reddit (`fetch` reads threads), 5ch.net (ja), pikabu.ru (ru), tieba.baidu.com (zh), zhihu.com (zh, walled), dcinside.com and clien.net (ko), cafe.naver.com (ko) |
| old or removed pages | web.archive.org: `fetch("https://web.archive.org/web/2024/<url>")` |

when one of these, or a site found on the way, carried the answer and has
no engine here, name it in the report: it may be worth adding to weltsuche.
